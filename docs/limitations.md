# Data coverage and limitations

## Available in the current release

- O\*NET 31.0 occupation records and six occupational profile dimensions, with source rating scales and metadata preserved.
- January 2026 CPS weighted retrospective occupation transition cells using the job-tenure and occupational-mobility supplement weight.
- A searchable occupational atlas, occupational profile inspection, and a transition matrix with weighted totals, denominators, and unweighted response counts.

## Not available in the current release

- Replicate-weight standard errors and confidence intervals. The official replicate-weight file has not been acquired and joined to the CPS records.
- Validated crosswalks from CPS 2018 Census occupation codes to O\*NET-SOC; transition cells therefore cannot be joined to occupational profiles.
- BLS employment projections and wage comparisons. No BLS workbook has passed acquisition and schema validation for this release.
- Profile-similarity scores, mobility pathways, network statistics, and occupational embeddings.
- Completed Mobility Rivers and Career Constellations visualizations, saved explorations, or transition exports.

The CPS estimates cover valid responses to a retrospective “same kind of work” question. They are not a complete longitudinal record of worker movement, do not capture every intermediate job, and are not unconditional career probabilities. A missing cell does not show that a transition never occurs. The release and its source register identify the available measures and missing inputs.
